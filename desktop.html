Full Page Screenshot & PDF for Windows

Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

In short

The Windows app takes and edits your screenshots entirely on your own computer. It does not send your screenshots, your screen, or anything about you to any server. It has no analytics, no ads and no account.

What the app does

Full Page Screenshot & PDF for Windows sits in the taskbar. When you click its icon, press its shortcut (Alt + Q by default) or choose Full screen, it takes a picture of your screen and lets you select an area. You can then copy it, save it, or open it in the built-in editor to annotate it (text, shapes, arrows, blur, callouts and more) and export it as PNG, JPG or PDF.

The screen is only captured when you start a screenshot. The app never records your screen in the background.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, send or share any data. It does not connect to the internet for its features, and it contains no analytics or tracking of any kind.

What we do not do

  • We do not upload your screenshots or any part of your screen anywhere.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone.
  • We do not run ads or include advertising or analytics code.
  • We do not require an account, sign-in or any personal information.

What stays on your computer

  • Screenshots you save go to the folder you choose (by default Pictures\Full Page Screenshot). Copied screenshots go to your clipboard.
  • History: your last 50 screenshots are kept in the app's own data folder on your computer so you can open, copy, save or delete them from History. You can delete any or all of them there at any time.
  • Settings (shortcut, save folder, editor options) are kept in the same app folder.
  • Start with Windows (on by default) adds the app to your own startup list. You can turn it off in Windows Settings under Apps > Startup. The version installed with Setup also has this option in its own Settings, and asks Windows once to show its icon on the taskbar instead of hiding it.

None of this is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

Third parties

None. The app does not use any third-party service.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not collect any personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published here with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
